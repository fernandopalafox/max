# 2026-05-05

## Adapter Regularization Losses

Implemented auxiliary pretraining losses for LoRA-XS based on the theory from 2026-05-01. The goal is to maximize the rank and conditioning of the adapter's Fisher Information Matrix at fine-tuning time.

Two losses added to a new `tdmpc2_regularized` trainer:
- **L_A** = ‖A^T A − I_r‖²_F — drives the output-side projection toward orthonormality
- **L_B** = ‖Ĉ − I_r‖²_F — whitens the bottleneck activations Bh across the batch

Also renamed LoRA-XS adapter matrices from P/Q to A/B to match paper notation (W_eff = W + B R A^T in row-vector code, equivalent to W + ARB in the paper's column-vector form).

Config: `configs/pretrain_loraxs_regularized_tdmpc2.json` (adapter_A_coef=1.0, adapter_B_coef=1.0).

Launched pretraining run: W&B `cheetah_ft_latent/zjftje1o`.
