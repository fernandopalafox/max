## Online Adaptation Experiments (r2)

Testing how well different pretraining strategies support online adaptation to a new environment (cheetah mass scale 0.1). All runs use TD-MPC2-style online learning (1 SGD step per collected transition), planner prefill (259 steps), no SGD burst, 500k steps, eval every 10k steps on 5 episodes.

The key question: does a meta-learned LoRA-XS subspace (FOMAML) adapt faster than a standard pretrained one, and how does parameter-efficient adaptation compare to full adaptation?

| Config | WandB run | Pretrained model | Dynamics | Trainer | What's trainable |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|----------|---------|-----------------|
| adapt_full_pt.json | full_pt_r2 | baseline_new/run_2 | dense | tdmpc2 | everything |
| adapt_loraxs_pt.json | loraxs_pt_r2 | baseline_loraxs/run_2 | dense_lora_xs (rank 48) | tdmpc2frozen | LoRA-XS R adapters only |
| adapt_loraxs_pt_fomaml.json | loraxs_pt_fomaml_r2 | baseline_loraxs_fomaml_bgd/run_2 | dense_lora_xs (rank 48) | tdmpc2frozen | LoRA-XS R adapters only |

Goal: determine whether BGD-FOMAML meta-pretraining consistently improves online adaptation vs standard LoRA-XS pretraining. Previous results showed a single instance of improvement that couldn't be reproduced — running 8 more seeds to get a statistically meaningful comparison.
