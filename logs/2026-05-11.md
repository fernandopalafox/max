# 2026-05-11

## Saved World Models Reference

All pretrained model directories currently on disk in `data/models/`. Paths are relative to `data/models/`. Each `run_N` folder contains `final.pkl` and (for 1M-step runs) step checkpoints every 100k.

### Cheetah

All runs: cheetah env, mass_scale=1.0 during training, MPPI 512 samples × 6 iterations.

| Path | Trainer | Dynamics | Seeds (N runs) | Config | Notes |
|------|---------|----------|---------------|--------|-------|
| `cheetah/baseline_new/run_{1..6}` | tdmpc2 | dense | 6 (seeds 44–49) | `pretrain_dense_tdmpc2.json` | Standard TD-MPC2 dense baseline, 1M steps |
| `cheetah/baseline_loraxs/run_{1..10}` | tdmpc2 | dense_lora_xs r=48 | 10 (seeds 44–53) | `pretrain_loraxs_tdmpc2.json` | Standard TD-MPC2 with LoRA-XS dynamics, 1M steps. Runs 1–2 from Apr 15–16; runs 3–10 from Apr 28–29. |
| `cheetah/baseline_loraxs_regularized/run_{1..6}` | tdmpc2_regularized | dense_lora_xs r=48 | 6 | `pretrain_loraxs_regularized_tdmpc2.json` | LoRA-XS + orthonormality loss L_A + whitening loss L_B (coeff 1.0 each), 1M steps. Achieves A^T A ≈ I and lower C_hat off-diagonal vs unregularized. |
| `cheetah/baseline_loraxs_reg_Cfix/run_{1..4}` | tdmpc2_regularized_Cfix | dense_lora_xs r=48 | 4 | `pretrain_loraxs_reg_Cfix_tdmpc2.json` | Same as regularized but C_hat estimated from t=0 batch activations only (not full horizon). Slightly worse geometry than regularized in practice. 1M steps. |
| `cheetah/grad_capture/run_{1..4}` | tdmpc2 (grad_capture) | dense | 4 (seeds 1–4) | `pretrain_grad_capture_tdmpc2.json` | Dense baseline, 1M steps. | `cheetah/grad_capture_first/run_{1..4}` | tdmpc2 (grad_capture) | dense | 4 (seeds 1–4) | `pretrain_grad_capture_tdmpc2.json` + `--max-steps 100000` | Training stopped at 100k steps. `final.pkl` is the model at step 100k. `g_sum_*` accumulates only the first 100k gradient steps. W_final for the full-run is taken from the corresponding `grad_capture/run_N` when running `analyze_subspace.py`. |
