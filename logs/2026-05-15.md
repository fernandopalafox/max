## Bottleneck Decomposition Experiment

### Motivation

On May 12 we ran OGD adaptation (dynamics-only, frozen encoder) on CartPole 5× pole. It plateaued at ~490 regardless of data quantity (500k vs 50k transitions performed the same at equal compute). The skyline (training from scratch) is 974. Something structural is blocking adaptation — the question is which component.

Two hypotheses: (1) the encoder maps 5× pole observations into a latent space shaped for 1× physics, so the dynamics model is trying to learn transitions in the wrong space; (2) the value function and policy are miscalibrated for the new task, degrading planner performance at eval even if the world model is correct.

To isolate which components actually need to change, we run two ablations on the oracle buffer (500k transitions; data quantity doesn't matter per May 12 results):

### Planned Runs

| Experiment | Buffer | Unfrozen | Frozen | Trainer | W&B group | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Encoder-only, full data | 500k | encoder | dynamics, critic, policy, reward | `ogd` | `encoder_only_fulldata` | Does fixing the latent space alone close the gap with sufficient data? |
| Encoder-only, low data | 50k | encoder | dynamics, critic, policy, reward | `ogd` | `encoder_only_lowdata` | Same question with limited data |
| Full TDMPC2, full data | 500k | all | — | `tdmpc2` | `tdmpc2_fulldata` | Upper bound of offline adaptation |
| Full TDMPC2, low data | 50k | all | — | `tdmpc2` | `tdmpc2_lowdata` | Does data quantity matter when everything is unfrozen? |

All: 4 runs (one per `baseline_new` source model), 400k SGD steps, eval every 50k steps, no model saving. W&B project: `finetune_cartpole`.

**Interpretation key:** If encoder-only ≈ full TDMPC2, the encoder was the bottleneck and the dynamics model was fine — low-parameter adaptation is viable, just targeting the wrong component. If encoder-only ≪ full TDMPC2, multiple components matter and a richer adaptation strategy is needed. Comparing fulldata vs lowdata within each condition tells us whether data quantity starts mattering once more components are unfrozen.
